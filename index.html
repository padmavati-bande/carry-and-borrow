Add and subtract with carrying

Addition

  1. Add the ones column.
  2. If the total is 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry the 1 to the next column.
  3. Add the next column, plus the carried 1. Keep going left.

Subtraction

  1. If the top digit is smaller than the bottom one, borrow: add 10 to it.
  2. Take 1 away from the digit to its left.
  3. Now subtract the column. Keep going left.
